Safety First

Electrical Safety Practices

Electrical safety is paramount. Follow these general educational guidelines to maintain a secure environment.

Avoid Overloaded Sockets

Do not plug too many high-wattage appliances into a single outlet or extension cord to prevent overheating.

Inspect Damaged Wires

Regularly check cords and cables for frayed insulation or exposed copper, and replace them promptly.

Keep Equipment Dry

Ensure electrical switches, sockets, and devices remain away from moisture, water leaks, and damp areas.

Use Circuit Protection

Ensure appropriate circuit breakers and residual current devices are installed to protect against electrical faults.

Do Not Handle Exposed Wiring

Never touch exposed live wires or attempt internal panel work without turning off the main power supply.

Contact a Qualified Professional

For complex installations, panel repairs, and major electrical work, always rely on a qualified professional.
